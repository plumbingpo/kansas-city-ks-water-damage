Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. The five below are ones we see constantly in Olathe, listed from most pressing to least.

Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months.
